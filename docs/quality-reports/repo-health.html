LOW Gradle daemon heap tuned without a metaspace bound (1 file)

1 gradle.properties file(s) set org.gradle.jvmargs with an explicit -Xmx heap bound but no -XX:MaxMetaspaceSize=. Overriding org.gradle.jvmargs replaces Gradle's default JVM args, dropping its default 256m metaspace cap.

Why it matters: A heap-tuned Gradle daemon with no metaspace bound can grow metaspace unbounded and OOM (java.lang.OutOfMemoryError: Metaspace) on large multi-module builds — an intermittent, machine-dependent build failure that is hard to diagnose.
Impact: Risk of intermittent Gradle daemon OutOfMemoryError (Metaspace) on large builds. Confidence: review_needed
Evidence (1 location)
  • gradle.properties — org.gradle.jvmargs has -Xmx but no -XX:MaxMetaspaceSize=
Add an explicit metaspace bound to org.gradle.jvmargs, e.g. `-XX:MaxMetaspaceSize=512m` alongside your -Xmx value.
